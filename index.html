Our story · Kitwe, Zambia

Quality sound.
Global vibes.

Founded in 2020 as Hi-Tech Studio, High-Tech Entertainment has grown into an independent creative company connecting music production, artist development, digital promotion and software development. Led by producer and CEO L Junior ZM (L Junior Zambia), HTE helps ideas move from the first beat to a finished release.
