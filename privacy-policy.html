High-Tech Entertainment

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

In brief: High-Tech Entertainment provides account access, music and video discovery, streaming, downloads, bookings and Founder Studio administration. Depending on the features enabled in a release, the service may process account details, user-submitted content, booking information, playback activity and advertising-delivery data. Audio promotions may be delivered as part of the listening experience.

1. Who operates the app

The High-Tech Entertainment mobile app is operated by High-Tech Entertainment (“HTE”, “we”, “us”). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Android app.

2. Information we process

  • Account information: If you register or sign in, your email address and a Supabase authentication user identifier are processed to create and manage your account.
  • Authentication information: Your password is submitted to Supabase Authentication for secure authentication. HTE does not receive or store your password in plain text.
  • Service and security data: Our backend provider may process technical request information, such as connection and security logs, to operate and protect the service.
  • Public catalogue information: The app retrieves published artist profiles, release information, track details, artwork and video links from the HTE catalogue. This is content displayed by the service, not information collected from your phone.

3. Why information is used

We use account information to register users, authenticate sign-ins, maintain account sessions, protect the service and respond to account-related requests. Catalogue information is used to display artists, music, releases and videos.

4. Service providers and sharing

The app uses Supabase as its backend and authentication service provider. Account and service data are processed by Supabase to provide the app's database and authentication functions. We do not sell personal information. When you open an external video link, that content is handled by the external website or app and its own privacy policy applies.

Supabase privacy information: supabase.com/privacy.

5. Advertising, analytics and device permissions

High-Tech Entertainment may deliver audio advertisements or promotional messages configured by its administrators. The app may record limited playback or advertising events where those features are enabled, to support service operation and campaign reporting. Availability of these features can vary by release. The app requires internet access for authentication, catalogue content, streaming, uploads, bookings and updates. Android may request file/media access when you choose content to upload or download. Permissions actually requested by your installed release are shown by Android system settings.

6. Voice Companion and microphone

The native Android Voice Companion may request microphone permission when you choose to press its microphone button. Its offline mode uses Android on-device speech recognition and locally installed Text-to-Speech voices that report no network requirement. In this offline mode, speech recognition and spoken responses are processed on the device and are not sent to HTE servers. The initial Voice Companion uses press-to-talk; it does not continuously listen for a wake word. You can deny microphone permission and continue using the rest of the app without voice commands.

7. Data security and retention

Network connections to the backend use HTTPS. Supabase provides the infrastructure used for authentication and data storage. We retain account information while an account remains active and for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and protect the service. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Account deletion and your choices

You can request deletion of your High-Tech Entertainment app account and associated personal information. Open the Account Deletion page and follow the instructions. You can also reach the website contact form at High-Tech Entertainment official website. We may ask you to verify that you control the account before processing a request. Some information may be retained where the law requires it or where it is necessary for legitimate security or legal purposes.

Do not send your password when requesting deletion.

9. Children's privacy

The app is a general music and entertainment service and is not specifically designed for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way that should not have occurred, contact us through the website so we can review the request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published at this address with a revised effective date.
