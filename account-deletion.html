HIGH-TECH ENTERTAINMENT

Request account deletion

You can ask High-Tech Entertainment to delete the account you created in the Android app and the personal information associated with it.

How to request deletion

  1. Open the High-Tech Entertainment contact page.
  2. In the subject, write Account deletion request.
  3. In your message, state that you want your High-Tech Entertainment app account deleted and provide the email address used to register.
  4. Submit the form. We may contact you to verify that you control the account before processing the request.

Do not send your password.

What is deleted?

After verification, we will delete or anonymise the app account and associated personal information that we control, unless we must retain particular information for legal, security or dispute-resolution reasons. Public artist, track, release and video catalogue content is not personal account data and may remain available.

Need more information?

Read our Privacy Policy. You can submit a deletion request without signing in to the app.
