# HTE Universe brand assets

- `high-tech-logo.svg`: crowned HTE emblem used as the square website favicon and compact identity mark.
- `website/public/images/brand/high-tech-wordmark.svg`: horizontal crowned emblem and High-Tech Entertainment wordmark used in the public website header.
- `website/public/images/brand/high-tech-hero.svg`: original cinematic black, metallic gold and silver homepage banner artwork; used in the homepage hero.
- `website/public/images/brand/hte-social-preview.png`: branded 1200×630 PNG used by Open Graph and Twitter/X social preview metadata.
- `website/public/images/brand/apple-touch-icon.png`: 180×180 raster icon for iOS home-screen bookmarks.
- `hte-universe-native/app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_launcher_foreground.xml`: Android adaptive launcher foreground, scaled into the launcher safe zone.
- `hte-universe-native/app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_launcher_monochrome.xml`: Android themed-icon foreground.

Brand palette: black, metallic gold, and silver. Public product name: HTE Universe. Tagline: Music. Beats. Production. Beyond.
